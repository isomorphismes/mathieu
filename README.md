# Mathieu

Original sources for the multiply transitive permutation groups discovered by Émile Mathieu.

## Original papers (scanned primary sources)

1. **1860** — Émile Mathieu, *Mémoire sur le nombre de valeurs que peut acquérir une fonction quand on y permute ses variables de toutes les manières possibles*, *Journal de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées*, 2e série, **5**, 9–42.
   - [Original paper, PDF preserved in this repository](papers/Mathieu-1860-Nombre-de-valeurs.pdf)
   - [Numdam source scan](https://www.numdam.org/item/JMPA_1860_2_5__9_0.pdf)
   - [Bibliographic record](https://www.numdam.org/item/JMPA_1860_2_5__9_0/)
   - Introduces cyclic substitutions and multiple transitivity, the starting point for Mathieu's later constructions.

2. **1861** — Émile Mathieu, *Mémoire sur l'étude des fonctions de plusieurs quantités, sur la manière de les former et sur les substitutions qui les laissent invariables*, *Journal de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées*, 2e série, **6**, 241–323.
   - [Original paper, PDF preserved in this repository](papers/Mathieu-1861-Memoire.pdf)
   - [Numdam source scan](https://www.numdam.org/item/JMPA_1861_2_6__241_0.pdf)
   - [Bibliographic record](https://www.numdam.org/item/JMPA_1861_2_6__241_0/)
   - Develops the multiply transitive construction, including the fivefold transitive action on 12 points; announces the 24-point case.

3. **1873** — Émile Mathieu, *Sur la fonction cinq fois transitive de 24 quantités*, *Journal de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées*, 2e série, **18**, 25–46.
   - [Original paper, PDF preserved in this repository](papers/Mathieu-1873-Fonction-cinq-fois-transitive-24.pdf)
   - [Numdam source scan](https://www.numdam.org/item/JMPA_1873_2_18__25_0.pdf)
   - [Bibliographic record](https://www.numdam.org/item/JMPA_1873_2_18__25_0/)
   - Supplies the explicit 24-point construction, now associated with M₂₄.

These are Mathieu's writings, not modern retellings. Both are in French. The digitizations are provided by Numdam / Mathdoc and the Bibliothèque nationale de France's Gallica program; preserve that provenance.

The [`papers/`](papers/) directory holds copies of all three complete scans, imported directly from the sources above. [`papers/SHA256SUMS`](papers/SHA256SUMS) records their SHA-256 hashes. Do not replace the originals with generated transcriptions or paraphrases.

## Permutations as movement

A related existing interaction is [coefficient-root-dance](https://github.com/isomorphismes/coefficient-root-dance): coefficients on one side, roots on the other, and continuous dragging with root tracking. It is a useful *visual and interaction precedent* for showing permutations as choreography.

**The distinction matters:** generic polynomial coefficient loops produce root braids and permutations; this does not by itself implement a Mathieu group. For Mathieu, animate exact generators acting on 11, 12, 22, 23, or 24 points, and independently check the subgroup and transitivity invariants.

For subsequent context, Robert L. Griess Jr., [*Twelve Sporadic Groups* (1998)](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-662-03516-0), discusses the Mathieu groups, Golay codes, and their relationship to later sporadic groups. That copyrighted book is a secondary reference, **not** an original Mathieu paper.
