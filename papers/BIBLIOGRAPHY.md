# Papers behind Griess's *Twelve Sporadic Groups*

This bibliography follows the Mathieu-group, Golay-code, Steiner-system and permutation-generator references in Robert L. Griess Jr., *Twelve Sporadic Groups* (Springer, 1998), [DOI 10.1007/978-3-662-03516-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-662-03516-0). Its [published bibliography](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/bbm%3A978-3-662-03516-0/1) occupies pp. 154–160.

**Access conventions:** **local scan** means a complete, unmodified historical PDF committed under `papers/`; **external PDF** means a full-text source held elsewhere, not copied here; **DOI/catalogue** means a citation and landing page, *not* a claim of free full-text access. Published works are linked rather than rehosted unless redistribution rights are clear. The three Mathieu originals are in the public domain; preserve the original Numdam/Gallica attribution and PDF bytes. Check `SHA256SUMS` for the committed scan hashes.
