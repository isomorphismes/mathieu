# Small Tarot markers

The 2026-10-21 presentation revision uses Moonrise Kingdom's `Moonrise2`
palette, listed in [karthik/wesanderson](https://github.com/karthik/wesanderson/blob/master/R/colors.R):
ochre `#C27D38`, sage `#798E87`, dark brown `#29211F`, parchment `#CCC591`.
The warm paper background is `#F3EEDF`. All suit glyphs are original small
line drawings in the native renderer; no card illustrations or image assets
are copied.

| Suit | M₂₄ dancers | Color |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wands | 1–6 | Ochre |
| Cups | 7–12 | Sage |
| Swords | 13–18 | Dark brown |
| Pentacles | 19–24 | Parchment |

Within each suit the six forms are circle, square, diamond, triangle,
hexagon and shield. M₁₂ uses three forms per suit, with dancers 1–3, 4–6,
7–9 and 10–12 respectively. A form and suit belong to the dancer and travel
with it under every permutation. They do not redefine ATLAS coordinates or
the 4×6 visualization layout.

Markers have an eight-unit radius in the existing 360×720 logical canvas,
versus eleven previously. Optional dancer and position numbers use a separate
3×5 alphabet at 0.6 units per cell. Selection and Witt-block membership use
thin rings. `SUITS` switches to the optional dot view; that view uses six
quiet tints within each suit so all 24 identities remain distinct by color.

Package identity and the permanent development signer are preserved.
VersionCode 2 / 0.1.1-development is a replacement update for the C67-tested
versionCode 1 candidate. The user's versionCode 1 launch report and screenshot
are physical C67 observations; they do not accept this new renderer or A1.
The managed Grease-route and independent producer-policy gates remain open.
